## Architectural Findings from Plists

### 1. HeadBoard Default App Grid (`DefaultOrder.plist`)
The default tvOS Home Screen is ordered as a hierarchical folder structure (`HBRootFolderKey`):
- **Dock Row (GridOrder 1000 - 5000)**: Prime real estate apps with interactive Top Shelf extensions:
  1. `com.apple.TVWatchList` (Apple TV App / Up Next)
  2. `com.apple.TVMusic` (Apple Music)
  3. `com.apple.Arcade` (Apple Arcade)
  4. `com.apple.TVPhotos` (Photos & Shared Albums)
  5. `com.apple.TVAppStore` (App Store)
- **App Grid (GridOrder 6000 - 11000)**:
  6. `com.apple.podcasts`
  7. `com.apple.TVMovies` (iTunes Movies)
  8. `com.apple.TVShows` (iTunes TV Shows)
  9. `com.apple.TVSearch` (Siri & Spotlight Search)
  10. `com.apple.TVHomeSharing` (Local Media Streaming)
  11. `com.apple.TVSettings` (System Preferences)

### 2. PineBoard System Compositor Entitlements (`pineboard-Entitlements.plist`)
PineBoard holds supreme display and window management privileges:
- `com.apple.QuartzCore.displayable-context`: Direct CoreAnimation frame scheduling and hardware layer compositing.
- `com.apple.QuartzCore.global-capture` & `secure-capture`: Screen recording, AirPlay mirroring, and secure DRM display plane management.
- `com.apple.BTServer.supportsPairing` & `programmaticPairing`: Low-level Siri Remote / Bluetooth accessory handshake.
- `com.apple.appletv.pbs.allow-siri` & `allow-reboot`: Hardware button interception and power state machine execution.
